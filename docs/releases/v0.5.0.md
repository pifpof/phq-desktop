PositioningHQ Desktop is a free, open-source app that guides you through building a positioning and competitive-intelligence wiki, using your own Anthropic API key. Everything stays in a folder you choose on your computer.

## New in 0.5.0: run research one step at a time

- **The Research page.** A new Research item in the sidebar lists every research step with its status, what it produces and what it costs. Run one step, tick a few, or run everything that is left. Open each result in the Wiki, come back, and run the next. You never have to pay for the whole run before you see whether it is useful.
- **Steps run in order.** A step can be ticked once what it needs has output, or when you tick it together. Pages that already exist are updated through Regenerate, with its preview, so your edits are never overwritten.
- **Add steps to a run that is going**, pause it, or stop it, from the same page.
- **A clear finish.** When your playbook is published you see it: sections, sources cited, competitors profiled, cost, and the biggest gap to close, then the playbook opens. If you are busy elsewhere, nothing moves: a note waits for you and the sidebar says Ready.
- **While you wait:** a progress bar, what is happening now, the time left, and what has been found so far (your homepage headline, competitors, reviews, each section's first takeaway). Read a section as soon as it is written.
- **Add proof while you wait.** Drop customer quotes, case studies and reviews during a run. They are kept aside, then added to your playbook when you choose.
- **Onboarding** now offers Run everything, Choose steps, or Not now.
- Fixed: a value driver's title no longer repeats inside its card, in the page, the PDF and Copy as markdown, including older playbook versions.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) | `PositioningHQ-0.5.0-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `PositioningHQ-0.5.0-x64.dmg` |
| Windows | the `.exe` installer |
| Linux | the `.AppImage`, or the `.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu |

You need an Anthropic API key. A full research run typically costs a few dollars on your account, with a budget cap you set. Single steps cost cents.

## First launch

These builds are not signed yet, so your computer will warn you the first time.

- **Mac:** open the app once and close the warning. Then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** on the SmartScreen message, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x`), then run it.
