PositioningHQ Desktop is a free, open-source app that guides you through building a positioning and competitive-intelligence wiki, using your own Anthropic API key. Everything stays in a folder you choose on your computer.

## New in 0.4.0: a redesigned Positioning Playbook

- **Step by step.** The playbook opens on an At a glance overview, then one tab per section (Market, Customers, Value, Advantages, Positioning, Competitors, Proof), with a progress bar. No more one long scroll.
- **Key takeaways** at the top of every section, and a **Gap to close** where the evidence is weak, with a concrete next step. Playbooks made before 0.4.0 can add them in one small run (about $0.10), which you review before anything changes.
- **Easier to read.** Value drivers, distinct advantages and competitors are cards you can open; the positioning statement is a table with copyable message variations.
- **This section panel:** confidence, sources, last changed, Make a change, Copy this section, and Ask Chat about it.
- **Export PDF:** a branded PDF with a cover, the overview, contents and every section. You can leave out material from your own files before sharing it.
- **Help plan:** collect the gaps you want help with and send them to the Parallel79° team through a form in your browser. The app sends nothing on its own.
- Fixed: value driver headings no longer appear twice.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) | `PositioningHQ-0.4.0-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `PositioningHQ-0.4.0-x64.dmg` |
| Windows | the `.exe` installer |
| Linux | the `.AppImage`, or the `.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu |

You need an Anthropic API key. A full research run typically costs a few dollars on your account, with a budget cap you set.

## First launch

These builds are not signed yet, so your computer will warn you the first time.

- **Mac:** open the app once and close the warning. Then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** on the SmartScreen message, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x`), then run it.
