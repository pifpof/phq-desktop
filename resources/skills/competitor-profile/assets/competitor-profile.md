### Competitor profile

Output as:
```markdown
# [Competitor Name]

## Executive summary
Three dense sentences: who they are, who they sell to, and how they win.

## Firmographics
- **Website:**
- **HQ:**
- **Size:** employees, funding or ownership
- **Primary target:** industries and company size

## Positioning
- **Hero message:** "exact homepage headline"
- **Problem they claim to solve:**
- **Category they claim:**
- **Recent messaging shifts:** changes in the last 12 months, if any

## Core features
Three to five features, each with the outcome they promise.

## Pricing and packaging
Model (seat, usage, flat, freemium), entry price if public, and where the number came from.

## Market sentiment
- **What users love:** with a short quote and source
- **What users complain about:** with a short quote and source

## Go-to-market
Where they are most active (LinkedIn, SEO, events, partners, product-led) and how they sell.

## Where they are weak
Gaps a competitor could use. Facts, not opinions.
```
