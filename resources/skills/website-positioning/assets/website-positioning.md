### Website positioning audit

Describe how the company positions itself on its own website.

Output as:
```markdown
# Website Positioning

## Homepage hero
- **Headline (H1):** "exact text"
- **Subhead:** "exact text"
- **Primary call to action:** "exact button text"

## Category claimed
What the site says the product is, in its own words, with the quote.

## Audience named
Who the site says it is for: roles, company types, industries. Quote the lines that name them.

## Value claims
The main benefits the site promises, most prominent first. Quote each one and give the page.

## Proof shown
Customer logos, numbers, case studies, testimonials, awards. List what is there and where. Write "None shown" if there is none.

## Repeated phrases
Words and phrases the site uses again and again, with a rough count.

## Tone
Three to five words for the voice, with one example line.

## Pages read
The page paths this audit is based on.
```
