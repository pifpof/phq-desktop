### Competitor list

Output as:
```markdown
# Competitors

| Name | Website | One line | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Example Co | example.com | What they sell, in one line | Direct |

## Why these
- One bullet per competitor: why a buyer would consider it instead.
```

Rules for the table:
- One row per alternative, three to five rows.
- **Type** is exactly one of: Direct, Indirect, Status quo.
- For a Status quo row, put "n/a" in Website.
- Website is the bare domain, such as example.com.
